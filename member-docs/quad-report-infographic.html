Your first run: have these nearby

20–40 MINUTES · SAVES AS IT GOES

  • P&L by month, last 12 months, accrual basisOr connect your accounting system. A column per month.
  • Balance sheet as of last month endFor your cash reserve.
  • Your time or job costing systemHours by person, by job, with a cost rate: Harvest, BigTime, Procore, ServiceTitan, QuickBooks Time. Optional; it makes margin real.
  • Your annual planning deckSo your goals go in word for word.

Expect a finding. The usual one: salaried delivery time in operating expenses, which flatters gross margin.
