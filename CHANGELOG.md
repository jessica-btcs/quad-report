# Changelog

Written for the owner, not for developers. Each entry says what changed and
whether they need to do anything.

## 1.0.3

- Fixed: when asked for a change, the AI could build its own slide and put
  the quadrants back in normal reading order. The slide now always comes from
  the Quad Report's own builder, in Scot's layout, and changes are made by
  rebuilding it.
- New monthly check-in: before the slide is built, it asks one question at a
  time about what's behind your numbers, where each goal stands, what went
  well, what didn't, how each core strategy is moving, and what you want from
  the group. Answer out loud if you like; it
  trims your words to fit and reads them back before building. You can also
  dictate quadrant iv, and it types it in word for word.
- If you use ChatGPT: replace your Project's files and instructions with the
  new pack.

## 1.0.2

- ChatGPT is retiring custom GPTs, so on ChatGPT the Quad Report now lives
  in a Project you set up once from the ChatGPT pack. It stays private to
  your account. On the ChatGPT desktop app you can install it as a plugin
  instead, and it updates from the source like the Claude version.
- If you use ChatGPT: follow the SETUP.md in the new ChatGPT pack.

## 1.0.1

- Setup now asks for the exact exports up front: your P&L by month for the
  last 12 months on accrual basis, last month's balance sheet, and your time
  or job costing system if you have one, with examples of what counts.
- "Set up my quad report" now starts setup in every tool. On ChatGPT, first
  timers are no longer asked to attach files they do not have yet.
- Corrected the update steps for Claude desktop: turn on Sync automatically
  once and updates arrive on their own.
- Nothing for you to do.

## 1.0.0

- First release for the group. Your profile and history now live in your own
  "Quad Report Data" folder, so updates never touch them and you can switch
  between Claude and ChatGPT without losing a month.
- Trends and STUCK flags are worked out from your history automatically.
- A month that has already been reported is never overwritten without asking.
- Fixed: a goal marked done now shows DONE on the slide.
- Nothing for you to do.
