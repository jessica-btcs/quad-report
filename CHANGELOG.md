# Changelog

Written for the owner, not for developers. Each entry says what changed and
whether they need to do anything.

## 1.0.1

- Setup now asks for the exact exports up front: your P&L by month for the
  last 12 months on accrual basis, last month's balance sheet, and your time
  or job costing system if you have one, with examples of what counts.
- "Set up my quad report" now starts setup in every tool. On ChatGPT, first
  timers are no longer asked to attach files they do not have yet.
- Corrected the update steps for Claude desktop: turn on Sync automatically
  once and updates arrive on their own.
- Nothing for you to do.

## 1.0.0

- First release for the group. Your profile and history now live in your own
  "Quad Report Data" folder, so updates never touch them and you can switch
  between Claude and ChatGPT without losing a month.
- Trends and STUCK flags are worked out from your history automatically.
- A month that has already been reported is never overwritten without asking.
- Fixed: a goal marked done now shows DONE on the slide.
- Nothing for you to do.
