# Quad Report Data

This folder is yours. It holds everything the Quad Report knows about your
company, and nothing outside it keeps a copy.

- `quad-profile.json`: your company, how each number is defined, your targets
  in your own words, your goals and strategies. Written during setup and
  changed only when something real changes.
- `history/`: one file per month you have reported, named `2026-09.json` and
  so on. This is how next month knows your trends and which goals have been
  stuck. Do not edit these by hand; they are the record of what you reported.
- `out/`: your finished slides, one per month.

Updates to the Quad Report never touch this folder. If you switch from
ChatGPT to Claude or back, bring this folder and you lose nothing.

Keep it in your OneDrive, Google Drive or SharePoint so it is backed up.
